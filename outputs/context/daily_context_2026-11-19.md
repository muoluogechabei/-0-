## Myocardial Hmgcs2-Driven Ketogenesis Promotes Atrial Fibrillation in Diabetes.
**链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43046951/

**摘要**: Type 2 diabetes (T2DM) increases the risk of atrial fibrillation (AF). Elevated circulating ketone bodies (β-hydroxybutyrate [β-OHB]) in T2DM are considered hepatic in origin and benefit the heart via energy provision. Although we found ketogenesis enzyme Hmgcs2 (3-hydroxy-3-methylglutaryl-CoA synthase 2) upregulated in both atrial and ventricular tissues of T2DM mice, alongside increased cardiac and circulating β-OHB. Whether enhanced Hmgcs2 is related to AF, ketogenesis-dependent or independent, remained unclear.
