## Untargeted Metabolomic Analysis of Acute Kidney Injury After Maze-Inclusive Cardiac Surgery.
**链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43009145/

**摘要**: The maze procedure is considered as the standard surgical treatment for atrial fibrillation and might be an independent risk factor for postoperative acute kidney injury (AKI). This study aimed to investigate the metabolic alterations associated with AKI following maze-inclusive cardiac surgery using untargeted metabolomics.
