## CACNA2D2 Regulates Mitochondrial Function to Attenuate Fibrosis and Atrial Fibrillation via the cGAS-STING Pathway.
**链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43070174/

**摘要**: Atrial fibrillation (AF) is the most common sustained cardiac arrhythmia and is thought to be driven by atrial fibrosis. A previous study has identified the expression of calcium voltage-gated channel auxiliary subunit alpha2delta2 (<i>CACNA2D2</i>) is significantly downregulated in patients with AF, but its functional role remains unclear.
