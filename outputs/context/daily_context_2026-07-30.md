## The Role of Circulating Polyunsaturated Fatty Acids in Atrial Fibrillation and Atrial Cardiomyopathy: A Machine Learning-Informed Structural Modelling Approach.
**链接**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42520406/

**摘要**: Beyond their cardioprotective effects, polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFAs) have also been linked to arrhythmogenicity, raising concern among individuals predisposed to atrial fibrillation (AF), including patients with heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF). Here, we examined associations of circulating PUFA profiles and PUFA supplementation with AF prevalence and incidence.
